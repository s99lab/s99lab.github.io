## 2. Structure-Change Sensor (SC)

**Japanese title:** 構造変化センサー（Structure-Change Sensor）：時系列対話・文書における構造変化候補の検出とリアルタイム・ブックマーキング  
**Author:** S. Meta  
**Jxiv DOI:** [10.51094/jxiv.6215](https://doi.org/10.51094/jxiv.6215)  
**Submitted:** 2026-08-26  
**Published:** 2026-09-08

**Role in the archive:** A method for detecting and preserving **candidate structural-change points** in longitudinal dialogue, documents, and interaction records without converting detection directly into interpretation or causation.

**Archive page:**

- [Structure-Change Sensor (SC)](STRUCTURE_CHANGE_SENSOR.html)

The public method distinguishes:

> Detection ≠ Interpretation ≠ Transmission ≠ Causation  
> Candidate ≠ verdict

The retrospective sensor is supported diagnostically across multiple cases; the realtime extension is specified and in prospective trial use rather than presented as a validated measurement scale.

**Scope note:** The paper is not limited to human-AI dialogue. Its public formulation covers longitudinal dialogue, documents, and interaction records, while human-AI collaboration is one of its explicit research contexts.

## 3. Empirical Audit Architecture (EAA)

**Japanese title:** 実証監査アーキテクチャ（EAA）：複数AIによる証拠再構成を比較可能にする監査方法の開発と前向き検証  
**English title:** *Empirical Audit Architecture (EAA): Development and Prospective Validation of an Audit Method for Comparable Evidence Reconstruction by Multiple AI Systems*  
**Author:** S. Meta  
**Jxiv DOI:** [10.51094/jxiv.6148](https://doi.org/10.51094/jxiv.6148)  
**Published:** 2026-09-16

**Role in the archive:** An audit method for comparing evidence reconstruction by multiple AI systems while preserving original outputs, differences, uncertainty, and replayable audit conditions.

**Archive page:**

- [Empirical Audit Architecture (EAA)](EMPIRICAL_AUDIT_ARCHITECTURE.html)

EAA was developed through development cases and then applied to a new case after freezing the method specification. In the paper, “prospective validation” refers to application to a new case after method freeze; it does not imply full preregistration of the entire case-selection process.

**Primary distinction:** comparable reconstruction requires more than similar conclusions. Evidence scope, uncertainty, normalization, and execution conditions must remain inspectable.

---

## Relationship Between EAA and SC

EAA and SC are **distinct but complementary public methods**.

- **EAA** asks how multiple AI reconstructions of bounded evidence can be compared, tracked, replayed, and audited without erasing differences or uncertainty.
- **SC** asks where longitudinal records contain candidate structural changes worth preserving for later interpretation and review.

They should not be collapsed into a single framework, and publication of one does not validate the other.

Within the broader archive, they sit near the AAS / Structural Drift research track because both concern auditability across changing records, interpretations, and long-horizon human-AI work. They do not replace AAS, Reality Stabilizer, or the core concept map.
