## The question behind the archive

A convincing surface signal does not always tell us what structure remains underneath. The archive asks what can actually be traced, reviewed, retained or tested against reality.

The research is organized into **two distinct tracks**, with a cross-cutting concept and two separately published methods:

| Area | Central question | Where to begin |
|---|---|---|
| **Retained-Demand Audit Series** | Does visible use of a digital asset create a durable need to hold it? | [Short explanation](WHAT_IS_RETAINED_DEMAND.html) · [Research papers and materials](https://github.com/s99lab/retained-demand-audit-series) |
| **AAS / Tri-Layer Architecture** | How can human judgment, AI assistance, roles and external records remain distinguishable over long collaboration? | [Formation note](AAS_FORMATION_NOTE_EN.html) · [Research papers and materials](https://github.com/s99lab/aas-trilayer-ambient-alignment) |
| **Reality Stabilizer / Contact Pressure** *(cross-cutting)* | Does a coherent explanation also meet institutional, practical and other real-world constraints? | [Concept note](REALITY_STABILIZER_EN.html) · [Jxiv paper](https://doi.org/10.51094/jxiv.4696) |
| **Structure-Change Sensor (SC)** *(separate published method)* | Where might the structure of a long record have changed? | [Overview](STRUCTURE_CHANGE_SENSOR.html) · [Jxiv paper](https://doi.org/10.51094/jxiv.6215) |
| **Empirical Audit Architecture (EAA)** *(separate published method)* | How can evidence reconstruction across AI systems be compared and reviewed? | [Overview](EMPIRICAL_AUDIT_ARCHITECTURE.html) · [Jxiv paper](https://doi.org/10.51094/jxiv.6148) |

**SC and EAA are separate, related methods.** They are not additional research tracks or proof that AAS itself has been published on Jxiv.

## How to verify a claim

Read the **original DOI and paper** for a Jxiv research claim, the **track repository and relevant revision** for a research-series claim, or the **original note article** for an essay or structural-analysis claim. Use this introduction and the concept map for navigation, not as evidence of independent validation.

The public archive does not contain private prompts, internal evaluation logic, unpublished project state or operational procedures.
