        <div><span class="smeta-kicker">THE POINT OF THE ARCHIVE</span><h2 id="about-title">Keep more than<br>the final answer.</h2></div>
        <div><p>In research and work, the final explanation can survive while the evidence available at the time, the people who made decisions, and the assumptions that changed along the way become hard to recover.</p><p>This archive preserves public paths to questions, their context, and source material that can be inspected later. The research, personal essays, and structural analysis are connected, but they do not serve the same purpose.</p><a class="smeta-start-inline" href="WHY_THIS_ARCHIVE_EXISTS.html">Read why this archive exists →</a></div>

        <div class="smeta-section-heading"><div><span class="smeta-kicker">RESEARCH / WHERE TO BEGIN</span><h2 id="research-title">Choose a research path</h2></div><p class="smeta-section-desc">Start with two independent series. Then explore a cross-cutting concept and three separately published works.</p></div>
        <div class="smeta-start-tracks"><article class="smeta-start-track"><span class="smeta-branch-tag">SERIES 01 / DIGITAL ASSETS</span><h3>Retained-Demand Audit Series</h3><p>A digital asset may be used without generating durable demand to hold it. This research separates visible activity from inventory, collateral, and liquidity needs that might remain with operators. XRP is a stress-test case, not a price forecast.</p><div class="smeta-start-track-links"><a href="WHAT_IS_RETAINED_DEMAND.html">What is retained demand?</a><a href="RETAINED_DEMAND_EVIDENCE_CHECKLIST.html">Evidence checklist</a><a href="https://github.com/s99lab/retained-demand-audit-series">Original series on GitHub</a></div></article>
<article class="smeta-start-track"><span class="smeta-branch-tag">SERIES 02 / HUMAN × AI</span><h3>AAS / Tri-Layer Architecture</h3><p>How can human judgment, AI assistance, roles, and external records remain distinguishable through long collaboration? This track examines boundaries of responsibility, the provenance of decisions, and structural drift.</p><div class="smeta-start-track-links"><a href="AAS_FORMATION_NOTE_EN.html">Formation note</a><a href="WHAT_IS_STRUCTURAL_DRIFT.html">Structural Drift</a><a href="https://github.com/s99lab/aas-trilayer-ambient-alignment">Original series on GitHub</a></div></article></div>
        <article class="smeta-start-cross"><div><span class="smeta-kicker">CROSS-CUTTING IDEA</span><h3>Reality Stabilizer / Contact Pressure</h3></div><div><p>A coherent explanation may still fail to meet institutional rules, accountability, costs, or implementation limits. This is a shared lens across subjects, not a third research track.</p><a href="REALITY_STABILIZER_EN.html">Read the concept overview →</a></div></article>
